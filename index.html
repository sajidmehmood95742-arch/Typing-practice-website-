About FastFingers

FastFingers is a modern typing test application designed to help you improve your typing speed and accuracy. With a clean, intuitive interface and various practice modes, you can track your progress, identify areas for improvement, and challenge yourself against others on the leaderboard.

Our goal is to provide a fun and effective way to master your keyboard skills, whether you're a beginner looking to learn touch typing or an experienced typist aiming for higher speeds.

Developed with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, FastFingers is a testament to responsive web design and user-centric development.
